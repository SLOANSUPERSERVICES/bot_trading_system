Connect Your Robinhood Account

Follow these steps exactly — takes about 5 minutes. Don't worry, you can't break anything!

1
Open Robinhood in your web browser

On your computer (not the phone app), open your internet browser and go to Robinhood.com. Log in with your username and password like normal.

2
Open the browser's developer panel

Once you're logged in, press these keys at the same time on your keyboard:

F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I (Mac: ⌘ Opt I)

A panel will open on the side or bottom of your screen — that's totally normal! It's just a tool that lets us peek at how the page is connected to Robinhood's servers.

3
Click "Application" (or "Storage") at the top of that panel

Look at the tabs at the very top of the panel that just opened. Click the one that says Application. On some browsers (Firefox) it says Storage.

💡 Tip: The panel might open at the bottom of your screen. You may need to scroll or look carefully for those tab labels.
4
Find "Local Storage" on the left side

On the left side of the panel, look for a section that says Local Storage. Click the little arrow next to it to expand it, then click on https://robinhood.com underneath it.

5
Find "activeSession" and copy your token

You'll see a big table with two columns: Key and Value. Look through the Key column for something called activeSession or search by pressing Ctrl+F and typing "Bearer". You'll see a very long string of letters and numbers that starts with eyJ — that's your secret token!

Click on that long string, then press Ctrl+A to select all of it, then Ctrl+C to copy it.

🔒 This token is like your password — it's private and only goes into the box below. Never share it in a chat or email.
6
Paste your token here and activate your bots

Paste your copied token into the box below. We'll store it securely in your .env file on your computer — it never gets uploaded anywhere.

Common Questions

I don't see "Application" — I see "Inspector" or something else.

If you're using Chrome, look for "Application." In Firefox, look for "Storage." In Safari, you may need to enable the Developer menu first: go to Safari → Preferences → Advanced → check "Show Develop menu." Then press ⌘+Option+I.

The Developer panel looks scary — will I break anything?

Not at all! Opening the Developer panel is completely safe. You're just looking at information — you can't accidentally change your account or delete anything just by having it open. Close it anytime by pressing F12 again.

I can't find "activeSession" or anything with "Bearer" in it.

Try refreshing the Robinhood page while the Developer panel is open. If you still don't see it, try clicking on "Network" tab at the top of the developer panel, then refresh the page, and look for any request to api.robinhood.com. Click on it and check the "Request Headers" section for "Authorization: Bearer eyJ..."

Is this safe? What does BotShare do with my token?

Your token is only ever saved to a file on YOUR computer — it never leaves your machine. BotShare (Cameron's system) runs entirely on your own computer using that token to talk to Robinhood's official API. Think of it like giving a house key to a trusted family member who's already in your home.

How often do I need to do this?

Robinhood tokens typically last about 24 hours. The bots will alert you when a new token is needed. You'll just paste a fresh one — takes less than 2 minutes once you know where to look!
